Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How does HapBeep work?

During Driving Mode, HapBeep listens for sounds and uses recognition to trigger haptic patterns on your Apple Watch. Detection is not guaranteed.

Why am I not receiving haptic alerts?

When safely parked, check that Driving Mode is active, microphone access is allowed, your watch is worn correctly and the relevant sound is enabled in HapBeep settings. Check your watch haptic settings too.

Why did HapBeep miss or misidentify a sound?

Background noise, distance and other conditions can affect sound recognition. Always independently verify your surroundings and never rely on an alert or its absence to make driving decisions.

What if the app stops working?

When safely parked, close and reopen HapBeep, check for available app and watchOS updates, and restart your watch if necessary. Contact support if the issue continues.

Where can I find the tutorial and settings?

When Driving Mode is off, use the question-mark button for the tutorial or the gear button for settings.
